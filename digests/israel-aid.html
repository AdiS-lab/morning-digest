Trump cuts $4 billion in Israel aid

back to digest · home

On election day, the administration announced it would end the annual $4 billion in U.S. aid to Israel. No explanation was given for the timing.

U.S. aid to Israel over time

The U.S. has provided military aid to Israel since the 1970s. The current $3.8 billion/year agreement was signed by Obama in 2016 as a 10-year memorandum of understanding. Additional aid packages during the 2023-2024 Gaza conflict pushed the annual figure above $4 billion.

What we don't know

  • Why it was announced on election day, when it would get the least coverage
  • Whether it's connected to the Iran situation
  • Whether this is a permanent policy change or a negotiating tactic
  • How Israel's government has responded (no official statement yet)
  • Whether Congress will try to override it, and whether the new Democratic majority would support or block the cut
