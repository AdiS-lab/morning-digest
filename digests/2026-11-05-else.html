Jordan: stabbing attack at embassy

A police officer was killed and another injured in a stabbing attack outside the Sri Lankan embassy in Amman, Jordan. The suspect was arrested. Jordanian authorities described it as a terrorist attack. No group has claimed responsibility.

Israel: new prime minister

President Isaac Herzog began consultations with party leaders to pick a prime minister-designate following the recent Knesset election. Opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot is the frontrunner. Netanyahu is fighting to hold on.

Markets

Global markets dropped on the U.S. election results. South Korea's KOSPI fell nearly 5% in early trading, led by semiconductor stocks. The reaction was driven by fears of tighter U.S. tech regulation under a Democratic Congress.
