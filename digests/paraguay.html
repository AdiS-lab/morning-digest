Paraguay's president is out after a bank collapse

back to digest · home

Regulators took over Ueno Bank early Tuesday, November 3. Deposits were frozen. Millions of Paraguayans were affected, including pensioners. Within three days, President Santiago Pena was out of office.

Pena was a shareholder in Grupo Vazquez, which owned Ueno, until March 2025. Diario Libre reports he kept financial ties to the bank after that. He is a close ally of Trump.

Three days

Pena first tried to contain it. He named a new central bank chief and proposed an emergency law to return savings. It was not enough.

The Senate vote

One report says 44 of 45 senators voted to remove him. Other reports say he resigned Thursday before that. This is unconfirmed. CNN describes him as under house arrest. Vice President Pedro Alliana is now president. Police arrested eight Ueno executives, including CEO Miguel Vazquez.
