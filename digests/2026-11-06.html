The Nasdaq dropped more than 5%. KOSPI fell over 8%, briefly dropping below 5,000. The trigger was the midterm results and growing fears of tighter data center regulation. Tech stocks took the worst of it. This continued into the next day with Nvidia losing $450 billion on November 7.

Democrats plan investigations into Trump's family

The party announced plans for comprehensive congressional investigations into the president's family and close associates, starting when they take control of both chambers in early 2027. This is expected. The party that wins midterms typically uses subpoena power. Clinton faced it in 1994. Bush faced it in 2006. Obama faced it in 2010. Trump faced it in 2018. And now again.

Hegseth impeachment effort advances

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth defended the Iran war and attacked his critics. The House is preparing to vote on articles of impeachment as early as next week. The effort is led by Kentucky's outgoing Republican representative Thomas Massie. Hegseth demanded $350 billion in additional military funding.

Cabinet impeachments are rare. The last time Congress impeached a cabinet member was Secretary of War William Belknap in 1876.

Alito won't retire

Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito has decided he will not retire until at least the end of the current court term. With Democrats taking the Senate, any retirement would mean a Democratic-controlled confirmation process. Alito is 76.
