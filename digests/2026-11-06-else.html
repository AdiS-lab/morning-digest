DSA mayors

At least four members of the Democratic Socialists of America won mayor's races in major U.S. cities on election day. The DSA is the largest socialist organization in the U.S., with roughly 90,000 members. For context, that's about the population of Wilmington, Delaware.

Paraguay's president resigned

President Santiago Pena resigned following the collapse of Ueno Bank, a major digital lender. Pena had business ties to the bank. Paraguay's vice president assumes the presidency. Ueno Bank was one of the largest digital-first banks in South America.

Trump's triumphal arch blocked

A federal judge halted construction of President Trump's proposed Washington triumphal arch shortly after a federal panel approved it. The arch was intended as a monument to the administration's achievements. The injunction cites environmental review requirements.

Russell Brand convicted

Comedian Russell Brand was found guilty of rape and three sexual assaults. The charges were brought in the UK. Brand had denied all allegations.

ACA premiums rising

As the Affordable Care Act marketplace opened, millions of Americans face double-digit increases to health insurance premiums. The average increase varies by state.
