Trump defended Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth on November 8, per CNN. He did so ahead of an expected impeachment vote. He also blamed GOP midterm losses partly on "bad messaging."

How we got here

Democrats won control of Congress in the midterms. Lawmakers are back for the lame-duck session. Incoming Democrats are weighing closer oversight of Hegseth, including impeachment. The coverage I found did not give a date for the vote.

What impeachment means

Impeachment is only the first step. Removal needs a two-thirds vote in the Senate.
