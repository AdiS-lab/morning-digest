Nvidia's board opened an independent probe into alleged foreign-intelligence targeting of CEO Jensen Huang. The probe follows a New York Times report about a link to a suspected Chinese spy.

What is reported, and how firmly

Reports say Huang admitted an affair with the suspected spy in a letter to staff. Those details are not independently confirmed.
