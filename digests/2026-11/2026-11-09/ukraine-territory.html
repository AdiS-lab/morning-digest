Russia hit four substations. Winter is coming.

back to digest · home

Russia's military struck four electrical substations in Ukraine on Monday. The attacks are part of a long campaign to cripple the country's energy infrastructure before winter. Ukraine is racing to make repairs before cold weather makes outages life-threatening.

Attack and response

In response, Ukrainian forces hit a Russian oil refinery. Zelenskyy said the EU has activated an emergency funding mechanism to accelerate repairs. The U.S. and Europe together have pledged over $1 billion for power grid restoration in 2026.

A pattern since 2022

Russia began systematic attacks on Ukraine's power grid in October 2022. The strategy targets heating and electricity before winter to pressure the population and strain the government. Ukraine has rebuilt much of the damaged infrastructure over four years, but each winter brings new rounds of strikes.

The pace of international repair funding has increased each year. The 2026 pledge of over $1 billion is the largest single-year commitment yet.
