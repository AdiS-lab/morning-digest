Stephen Miller named White House chief of staff

Trump named Stephen Miller as his next chief of staff on Friday. Miller replaces Susie Wiles and starts Monday, November 9. Wiles moves to Trump's political operation. The change comes three days after Republicans lost control of Congress.

Miller has been deputy chief of staff for policy. He is best known for shaping the administration's immigration crackdown. He is Trump's sixth chief of staff across two terms.

U.S. carries out "minor strikes" on Iran

Trump said U.S. forces hit Iranian air defenses near the Strait of Hormuz on Friday. Iranian state media reported two explosions on Qeshm Island. These are the first strikes since the midterms. Trump called them minor. He also said "we've made a lot of progress with Iran."

Qatar is pushing a 48-hour plan to reopen the strait. Iran called the latest U.S. reply "unconvincing."

U.S. measles cases pass 5,000

The CDC confirmed 5,182 measles cases this year. That is the highest count since 1991. It is more than double last year's 2,289. Cases are spread across 45 states. States report eight deaths. The CDC recognizes four.

The U.S. has held measles elimination status since 2000. A regional health body reviews that status next week.

Paraguay's president is out after a bank collapse

Santiago Pena left office after regulators seized Ueno Bank and froze deposits. Pena was a shareholder in the bank's parent group until March 2025. Prosecutors opened a criminal investigation into him. Eight bank executives were arrested.

Reports conflict on the sequence. Some say he resigned Thursday. Others say the Senate removed him 44 to 1. Vice President Pedro Alliana has taken over, per CNN.

Nvidia fell 9.3% on Thursday, its worst day of the post-election sell-off. Marvell fell more than 16%. All 11 market sectors dropped. On Friday, both stocks recovered more than 4%.

Investors fear Democrats will slow data center construction. Nvidia reports earnings on November 17.
