U.S. carries out "minor strikes" on Iran

back to digest · home

Trump said U.S. forces knocked out an Iranian anti-aircraft system near the Strait of Hormuz on Friday. He said it took about 15 minutes, "maybe five." Iranian state media reported two explosions in the Suza area of Qeshm Island. Trump did not say directly that these were the same strikes.

This was the second round in a week. Sources differ on when the first one happened. Some say Monday, November 2. Others say early Tuesday. U.S. officials called the earlier strike "defensive." It reportedly followed an Iranian missile fired at a U.S. aircraft.

The week so far

The signals are mixed. On Thursday Trump said the U.S. may have to "destroy" the rest of Iran. On Friday he said "we've made a lot of progress."

Qatar's plan

Qatar is mediating. Its plan phases in steps over two days. The U.S. lifts the blockade and releases some frozen Iranian funds. Iran gives the IAEA access to struck sites. Iran then reopens the strait under a temporary arrangement. Neither side has accepted it publicly.

Iran says it sent a nuclear proposal through Qatar. It says that will only be discussed after the U.S. meets seven demands to reopen the strait. For background on why the strait matters, see the Strait of Hormuz page.
