U.S. measles cases pass 5,000

back to digest · home

The CDC confirmed 5,182 measles cases in 2026 as of Friday. That is the most since 1991. The year is not over. About 96% of cases are tied to outbreaks. Cases are in 45 states.

Cases by year

In 2000 the U.S. declared measles eliminated. That year saw 86 cases. The 2019 outbreak reached 1,274. Last year hit 2,289. This year has more than doubled that.

Deaths

State health departments report eight deaths. Six in Pennsylvania, one in New York, one in Wisconsin. The CDC recognizes four. It is working with outside epidemiologists to define what counts as a measles death.

What happens next

The Pan American Health Organization reviews U.S. transmission data next week. It will decide whether the U.S. keeps its elimination status. Losing it would mean measles is considered spreading continuously inside the country again.
