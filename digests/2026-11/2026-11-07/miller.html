Stephen Miller named chief of staff

Trump announced the change on Truth Social on Friday, November 6. Miller takes over Monday, November 9. Susie Wiles moves to Trump's political operation. Trump praised her service.

The timing follows the midterms. Republicans lost both chambers of Congress on November 3. Democrats take control in early 2027. See the midterms page and the investigations page for what that means.

Trump's chiefs of staff

Miller is the sixth person in the job across Trump's two terms. Four served in the first term alone. Mick Mulvaney was acting chief the whole time.

How long each one lasted

Wiles lasted longer than any of the first-term chiefs. About 22 months. Reince Priebus lasted about six.

Who Miller is

Miller was a senior adviser and speechwriter in the first term. In the second term he became deputy chief of staff for policy. He shaped the administration's immigration crackdown. Wiles was seen as a manager. Miller is seen as a policy driver.
