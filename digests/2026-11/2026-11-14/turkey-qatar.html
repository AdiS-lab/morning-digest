Turkey and Qatar sign mutual defense pact in Istanbul

What was signed

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan and Qatari Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani signed five bilateral agreements in Istanbul at the 12th meeting of the Turkey-Qatar High Strategic Committee. The centerpiece was a mutual defense pact committing each country to consult and coordinate in the event of a military threat to either party. The other four deals covered consumer protection standards, educational cooperation, a framework for joint conflict resolution initiatives, and a cultural exchange program.

The defense pact formalizes a security relationship that has grown steadily since Turkey opened a military base in Qatar in 2017. That base, which hosts roughly 3,000 Turkish troops, was expanded after the Gulf blockade of 2017 to 2021 and has since been used for joint exercises. The new pact elevates that arrangement to a treaty obligation, requiring formal consultation if Qatar faces a threat from a neighboring state.

Regional context

The pact comes as U.S. military operations against Iran have strained security arrangements across the Persian Gulf. Qatar hosts Al Udeid Air Base, the largest U.S. air installation in the Middle East, which has been used for strikes against Iranian targets. At the same time, Qatar has maintained economic and diplomatic channels with Tehran, and has pursued its own role as a mediator between the U.S. and Iran. The mutual defense pact with Turkey gives Qatar a security backstop that does not depend solely on the U.S. umbrella.

Turkey has pursued a parallel strategy, positioning itself as a mediator in both the Iran and Ukraine conflicts while maintaining defense exports and energy ties with both sides. Erdogan described the pact as a stabilizing measure. He said the two countries had agreed to establish a joint crisis consultation mechanism that could be activated within 48 hours of a threat assessment. The signing ceremony was attended by defense ministers and senior intelligence officials from both countries.
