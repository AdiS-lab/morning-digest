Iran strikes U.S. base in Kuwait. U.S. had hit an IRGC missile site a day earlier.

Wednesday's strikes

Iran's army claimed drone strikes before dawn on Wednesday against Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base, saying its forces targeted U.S. Patriot air defense batteries and missile launch infrastructure on the installation. Kuwait's Ministry of Defense said all incoming drones were intercepted by air defense systems and that no personnel or equipment sustained damage. Bahrain's defense forces intercepted a separate wave of Iranian drone attacks the same morning, also with no reported damage or casualties.

The attacks mark the first direct Iranian strikes on Kuwait in over two months, ending a period in which both sides had largely paused active combat operations in the Gulf. The expansion puts three countries, Bahrain, Kuwait, and U.S.-leased infrastructure at both, into direct contact with Iranian drone operations within roughly one week.

The U.S. strike on Qeshm Island

U.S. Central Command confirmed Tuesday that it destroyed an IRGC missile launch facility on Qeshm Island, at the mouth of the Strait of Hormuz. CENTCOM said the strike "was in response to IRGC aggression targeting American personnel and assets." Iranian state media confirmed damage to a launch site on the island but said no IRGC personnel were killed. U.S. officials said the Qeshm site had been monitored since late October, when intelligence indicated Iran was staging ballistic and cruise missiles there for use against Gulf-based American assets.

Wednesday's Iranian strikes on Kuwait appear to be a direct response to the Qeshm operation. The pattern fits the cycle established over the past two weeks: a U.S. precision strike against an Iranian military site is followed within 24 to 48 hours by Iranian drone or missile attacks on a Gulf partner base. Each Iranian response has targeted a new location, moving from maritime chokepoints to Bahrain's Riffa Air Base and now to Kuwait. Kuwait requested additional Patriot batteries from the United States on Wednesday. No American military response to the Kuwait attacks had been announced by end of day.
