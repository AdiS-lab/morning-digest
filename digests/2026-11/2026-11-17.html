Russia hits both Kyiv bridges. Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge.

Russia fired seven ballistic missiles at Kyiv overnight, striking the North and South bridges over the Dnipro River. Mayor Vitali Klitschko said both bridges are closed, parts of the Green Line metro are suspended, and water and power have been cut to several districts. At least 14 people were killed and 78 injured across Ukraine in the overnight strikes, which also hit Kharkiv, Dnipro, and Zaporizhzhia.

Ukraine responded by striking the Kerch Bridge connecting Russia to occupied Crimea. The Moscow-appointed head of Crimea said one arch and a section of roadway collapsed into the sea. Russia's Transport Ministry suspended both road and rail traffic on the bridge.

U.S. intelligence: Iran is planning a dirty bomb attack on Washington

Seven U.S. officials told CNN that intelligence indicates Iran is developing specific plans to detonate a dirty bomb or chemical weapon in Washington, D.C., targeting senior Trump administration officials. The planning is described as giving Iran options rather than signaling an imminent attack. Some officials said intercepted communications suggest bomb-making materials may already be inside the United States, though officials disagree on the credibility of that claim.

President Trump, speaking in Manila, said the U.S. is taking the threat "very seriously." The White House separately said there is no credible threat to the homeland. Iran's U.N. mission denied all the allegations.

Trump in Manila for ASEAN. Shenzhen trilateral set for Thursday.

President Trump arrived in Manila for the U.S.-ASEAN summit, the first stop of a two-city Asia trip. He will fly to Shenzhen on Wednesday to attend APEC, where a first-ever trilateral meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin is scheduled for Thursday. Trump confirmed nuclear disarmament and Iran will be on the agenda. Ukraine is not listed.

Ukrainian President Zelensky said he is concerned that decisions about Ukraine could be made without Kyiv present. U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner are scheduled to meet Ukrainian and European officials in Berlin on Thursday to work in parallel on security guarantees and postwar recovery.

Japan scrambles jets after Russian aircraft violate its airspace

Japan's Defense Ministry said two suspected Russian military aircraft violated Japanese airspace north of Hokkaido early Tuesday morning. Japan launched fighter jets in response and lodged a "strong protest" with Moscow through diplomatic channels. It is one of the most serious airspace violations Japan has recorded involving Russia.

The incident comes as Trump is in Manila meeting with ASEAN leaders and as the broader U.S.-Russia relationship shifts ahead of the Shenzhen summit. Japan's government said it is seeking an explanation from Russia and monitoring the situation closely.

U.K. sanctions eight Israeli companies over West Bank settlements

The U.K. government announced sanctions on eight Israeli construction companies involved in building settlements in the occupied West Bank. Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband said the action was taken ahead of the final deadline on a major settlement tender, the E1 project, which would bisect the West Bank and block a viable Palestinian state. Miliband said protecting the two-state solution remains the government's "North Star."

The U.K. also sanctioned one activist group that has been blocking aid deliveries into Gaza. Germany called on Israel separately to allow more humanitarian aid into Gaza before the winter months.
