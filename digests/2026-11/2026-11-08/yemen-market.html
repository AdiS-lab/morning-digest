Dozens killed at a Yemen market. Who struck it is disputed.

back to digest · home

On Friday, November 6, a market in Taiz province was hit. Houthi officials say at least 30 people died and about eight were hurt. AP called it one of the deadliest attacks since fighting resumed between the Houthis and Saudi-backed forces.

Taiz is mostly under Houthi control. That makes independent checks unlikely. The toll comes from Houthi officials.

Two accounts

Iran's Foreign Ministry condemned "Saudi-led airstrikes" on Sunday. Its statement put the toll at 33, including 13 children. That figure has not been confirmed either.

Not the first disputed market strike

Each of these drew competing claims. None has been independently confirmed.
