Russia-Ukraine talks set for Tuesday in Abu Dhabi

back to digest · home

The U.S. will host Russian and Ukrainian delegations in Abu Dhabi on November 10 and 11. Steve Witkoff announced the date. He and Jared Kushner are expected to lead the U.S. side. Reports describe the meeting as working-level talks.

The agenda covers post-war security, de-escalation, humanitarian issues and next steps toward peace.

Talks stopped for nine months

Three rounds were held early this year. Talks were suspended when the Iran war began in late February. This will be the fourth round.

Two tracks this week

U.S. negotiators also plan meetings in Brussels. They will meet France, Germany, the UK, the EU and NATO. Those talks cover security guarantees and rebuilding Ukraine.

The two sides remain far apart on terms. Zelensky has said the talks could be delayed if fighting in the Middle East escalates. Earlier coverage is on the Ukraine deal page.
