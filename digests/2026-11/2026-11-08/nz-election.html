New Zealand election is too close to call

back to digest · home

New Zealand voted on Saturday, November 7. Labour edged National on the party vote, 29.36% to 29.21%, in preliminary results. National won more electorate seats, so it leads on seats.

Seats by party, preliminary

ACT and NZ First both grew their vote share. That gives them more leverage in coalition talks.

The governing bloc sits just above a majority

National, ACT and NZ First together hold 67 of 131 seats, per CNN. A majority is 66. ABC's live blog reported 68 at an earlier count. Both figures are provisional. About 400,000 special votes are still uncounted, including votes from overseas. Special votes have shifted seats in past elections.
