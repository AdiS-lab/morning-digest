Qatar's Hormuz plan still has no takers

back to digest · home

Qatar submitted a two-stage plan to the U.S. and Iran on November 6. It would end the fighting and reopen the Strait of Hormuz within 48 hours. As of the latest reports, neither side has accepted it. Neither has rejected it.

How the plan would work

The size of the asset release is unclear. Reports cite both $8 billion and 6 billion pounds.

The past four days

Iran says it wants practical U.S. steps, not "paper commitments." On November 5 its top security official said Tehran would not reopen the strait until seven demands are met. They include lifting sanctions. Iran has exempted ships from India, China, Iraq and Qatar from its restrictions. Analysts say Iran is trying to separate shipping and oil exports from the nuclear question.
