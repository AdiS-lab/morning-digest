Russia and Ukraine agree to prisoner exchange as Abu Dhabi talks end

Two days of U.S.-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi closed Wednesday without a ceasefire agreement. Russia and Ukraine did agree to a large exchange of prisoners of war. Envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff flew to Moscow afterward for talks with Putin.

Ukraine said it will not cede occupied territory. The U.S. asked Russia to submit a new security proposal. Kushner and Witkoff are set to travel to Ireland next to meet Zelensky.

Three tankers catch fire in Strait of Hormuz. Bessent moves family to military base.

Three oil tankers caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday. Iran says the strait will stay closed until the U.S. responds to its seven conditions for peace talks. Iranian officials said two supertankers carrying 4 million barrels were disabled.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved his family to a military base. A senior administration official said the threats against Bessent had reached an advanced stage. At least 37 ships have been attacked near Hormuz in November alone.

Jim Jordan elected House Minority Leader

House Republicans chose Jim Jordan as their minority leader for the 120th Congress. He beat Steve Scalise 106 to 73. Jordan is a co-founder of the House Freedom Caucus and the first member of that group to lead the party in Congress.

Speaker Mike Johnson did not seek the minority leader role. Republicans lost their majority last week. Democrats now hold 253 seats to Republicans' 167, with a few races still uncalled.

Congo Ebola outbreak is second-largest on record

The Democratic Republic of the Congo confirmed an Ebola outbreak that is now the second largest in recorded history. The outbreak follows the original 2014 to 2016 West Africa epidemic in scale. Health officials are working to contain the spread.
