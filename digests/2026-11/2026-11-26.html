Iran's Hormuz blockade enters Day 5. Kuwait drone attack kills two Italian contractors. Houthis claim strike on Ras Tanura.

Iran's IRGC Navy maintained its effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz on Thursday, with IMF PortWatch recording near-zero commercial transits for a fifth consecutive day. Iranian drones struck a U.S.-linked facility at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait, killing two Italian contractors and wounding several others. Kuwait's defense ministry confirmed the attack and said it was coordinating with its allies. Iran's army commander reiterated that the strait would remain closed until Washington met Tehran's seven conditions, including a halt to U.S. naval operations in the Gulf and the release of frozen Iranian assets.

Iran's Houthi allies in Yemen separately claimed responsibility for a drone strike targeting Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura export terminal, though Saudi Aramco said operations were unaffected. Brent crude rose to $147 per barrel as shipping insurers flagged the Kuwait attack as an escalation beyond the strait itself. Qatari and Omani mediators were still attempting to bridge the gap between U.S. and Iranian positions. The U.S. Fifth Fleet in Bahrain said it was conducting freedom-of-navigation patrols in the southern Gulf.

Russia fires ballistic missiles and 113 drones at Ukraine overnight. At least three killed in Kharkiv.

Russia launched a large overnight barrage against Ukraine using Iskander-M and Zircon ballistic missiles alongside 113 Shahed-type strike drones. Ukrainian air defenses neutralized 87 of the drones, but hits were recorded at 20 locations and debris fell at six more. Residential buildings and an educational institution in Kharkiv were struck, killing at least three people and leaving others trapped under rubble. Power supply disruptions were reported in Kryvyi Rih following a separate attack on electrical infrastructure.

Ukraine's Defense Forces struck back, hitting the Subkhankulovo oil pumping station in Russia's Bashkortostan region, a chemical plant producing components for military explosives, and a power station in the Stavropol region. Ukraine's General Staff reported 167 combat engagements on the front lines over the preceding 24 hours. Russia and Ukraine also exchanged the bodies of fallen servicemen, with 250 returned to Ukraine and 22 to Russia through International Committee of the Red Cross mediation.

Trump meets NATO chief Rutte at Mar-a-Lago on Thanksgiving. Kushner says large prisoner exchange is coming soon.

President Trump hosted NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte at Mar-a-Lago on Thanksgiving, the first meeting between the two since Trump met Putin in Shenzhen a week earlier. Rutte told Fox News ahead of the meeting that keeping Ukraine in the fight was essential and that pressure on Putin appeared to be working. Trump and Rutte discussed allied support for Ukraine and options for increasing diplomatic pressure on Moscow. The White House said the two agreed that any ceasefire must include verifiable terms and not reward Russian territorial gains.

U.S. envoy Jared Kushner separately stated that trilateral talks were on track to produce the largest prisoner exchange of the war before the holiday season. Kushner described Putin as a political strategist but said the U.S. held more leverage than Moscow acknowledged. Zelensky said in a statement that Ukraine was waiting for Washington to set a date for a new round of peace talks. No timeline was announced from the Mar-a-Lago meeting.

Trump proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners under Section 301. China and EU push back.

The U.S. Trade Representative released findings from a monthslong Section 301 investigation on Wednesday, proposing new tariffs on 14 major trading partners the administration accused of maintaining excess industrial capacity that undercuts American manufacturers. The list includes China, the European Union, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, India, Vietnam, and several Southeast Asian economies. The legal basis is Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, which the administration chose after the Supreme Court struck down its earlier country-specific tariffs in February. Rates range from 15 percent for Japan and the EU to as high as 35 percent for Vietnam if no trade deal is reached.

China warned it reserved the right to take necessary countermeasures and said the new tariffs were unacceptable. The EU trade commissioner said Brussels would respond with proportionate measures if the proposal moved forward. India and Brazil also issued objections. The 14 economies targeted together account for roughly half of global economic output. The USTR said lower rates would apply to countries that reach or are actively negotiating trade agreements with Washington, and set a public comment period before any final rule.

Jensen Huang tells Nvidia staff the board reinstated him on its own. Trump disputes the account. Congressional hearings set.

Jensen Huang addressed Nvidia employees at an all-hands meeting on Tuesday, his first since the board voted unanimously to reinstate him as CEO eleven days after his resignation. Huang apologized to staff and said he was in daily therapy. He also told employees that Trump's public claim to have personally arranged his return was completely untrue and that the board acted on its own judgment. Huang said he had not asked the White House for any intervention. Trump had told CNBC he spoke directly and indirectly with the board and that his intervention was very important.

The contradiction sharpened political scrutiny of the episode. Attorney General Todd Blanche gave evasive answers at a press briefing on whether Huang remained under federal investigation for the espionage matter that led to his resignation. Some Republicans, including Senator Pete Ricketts, said Huang's conduct did not support reinstatement. Senate Majority Leader Schumer renewed calls for hearings. Huang is expected to testify behind closed doors before the House Intelligence Committee around December 1 and publicly before the Senate Banking Committee after that. Nvidia shares held near their post-reinstatement levels but were down slightly on the day.
