Trump offers Dana White the press secretary job

Trump said Saturday he offered the White House press secretary job to UFC chief Dana White. He made the offer on Truth Social. He also invited White to bring UFC fighters as assistants.

CNN reported, citing a source, that White declined. White has not confirmed that. He says he will meet Trump on Monday. One aide said Trump was joking. The job has been unsettled since Karoline Leavitt left in August.

New Zealand election is too close to call

New Zealand voted Saturday. Labour and National are nearly tied on the party vote, about 29% each. National won more seats, 42 to Labour's 36, in preliminary results.

The governing right-wing bloc has 67 of 131 seats, per CNN. A majority is 66. About 400,000 special votes are still uncounted. They could change the result.

Qatar's Hormuz plan still has no takers

Qatar's two-stage, 48-hour plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz is still on the table. Neither the U.S. nor Iran has accepted it. Neither has rejected it either.

Under the plan, the U.S. would lift its naval blockade and release some frozen Iranian assets. Iran would let nuclear inspectors in. It would reopen the strait on day two. Iran says it wants action, not "paper commitments."

Russia-Ukraine talks set for Tuesday in Abu Dhabi

U.S.-brokered talks between Russia and Ukraine are set for November 10 and 11 in Abu Dhabi. It is the fourth round this year. It is the first since talks stopped in late February, when the Iran war began.

Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner are expected to lead the U.S. side. The two sides remain far apart on terms.

Dozens killed at a Yemen market. Who struck it is disputed.

Houthi officials say airstrikes hit a market in Taiz province on Friday. They say at least 30 people died. They blame Saudi jets. Iran condemned the strike on Sunday.

Yemen's Saudi-backed government says no airstrikes happened there. It blames the Houthis. Neither account has been independently confirmed.
