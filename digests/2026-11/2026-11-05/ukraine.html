Trump says Ukraine deal is close

back to digest · home

The president said he met with a Putin envoy and sees a deal soon. He described it as potentially "a big, beautiful agreement" covering all of Europe. That's all that's confirmed. Here's what we know and don't know.

Timeline

What's been said

  • Trump claims direct contact with a Putin envoy
  • He framed the deal as covering "all of Europe," not just Ukraine
  • No specifics on territorial concessions
  • No confirmation from Ukraine or from Zelensky's office
  • No confirmation from the Kremlin either

What's not been said

What Ukraine gives up. Whether Crimea is on the table. Whether NATO membership is off the table. Whether this is a real negotiation or a public pressure campaign. Whether Zelensky has agreed to anything. The gap between what's claimed and what's confirmed is large.
