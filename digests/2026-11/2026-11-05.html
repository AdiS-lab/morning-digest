Strait of Hormuz: tankers hit mines

Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps said two oil tankers and a tugboat caught fire after attempting unauthorized routes through the Strait of Hormuz. One tanker struck a mine. Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei said Iran's control of the strait had shattered "the myth of America's invincibility."

About 20% of the world's oil passes through the Strait of Hormuz. It is 21 miles wide at its narrowest point.
