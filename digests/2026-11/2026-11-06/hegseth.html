Hegseth impeachment effort

back to digest · home

The House is preparing to vote on articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth as early as next week. The effort is led by outgoing Kentucky Republican Thomas Massie. Hegseth defended the Iran war and demanded $350 billion in additional military funding.

Cabinet impeachments in U.S. history

Cabinet impeachments are extremely rare. The only prior case was Secretary of War William Belknap in 1876, who was impeached for accepting bribes. He resigned before the Senate could convict. If Hegseth is impeached, he would be the second cabinet member in 250 years of U.S. history.

The $350 billion ask

Current U.S. defense spending is roughly $886 billion per year. Hegseth is asking for $350 billion on top of that. For context, the entire U.S. education budget is about $90 billion.
