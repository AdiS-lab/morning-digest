Alito won't retire

back to digest · home

Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito announced he will not retire until at least the end of the current court term. With Democrats taking the Senate, any vacancy would mean a Democratic-controlled confirmation process.

Current court by age and appointing president

The court is 6-3 conservative. Alito (76) and Thomas (78) are the oldest conservative justices. If either retires or dies while Democrats control the Senate, the balance shifts. That's why Alito is staying.

Strategic retirements

Justices time their retirements to let ideologically aligned presidents pick their replacements. Breyer retired under Biden. Kennedy retired under Trump. Ginsburg tried to wait for a Democratic president and died during Trump's term. Alito is not making Ginsburg's mistake.
