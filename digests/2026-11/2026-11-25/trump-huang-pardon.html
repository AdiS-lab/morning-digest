Trump says a pardon for Jensen Huang is "always an option." Governance critics push back.

November 25, 2026

Trump's pardon comment

President Trump told the New York Post in an interview published Tuesday that pardoning Jensen Huang was "always an option," one day after Trump confirmed he had called into Nvidia's board meeting to push for Huang's reinstatement as CEO. Huang has not been charged with any crime. The question of a pardon relates to potential legal exposure from the espionage investigation that led to his resignation eleven days earlier. Nvidia's stock, which surged 30 percent Monday on news of Huang's return, pared gains slightly after the pardon comment as traders cited uncertainty about the ongoing federal investigation.

Congressional and expert reaction

House Minority Leader Jim Jordan said the pardon comment was "premature" and that the espionage investigation should be allowed to run its course. Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer called for a congressional hearing on presidential interference in corporate governance. Corporate governance experts said Trump's boardroom call and pardon suggestion together represented an unprecedented blurring of government and private enterprise. Criticism came from both sides of the aisle, with several Republican senators expressing unease about the optics even as they stopped short of condemning the president directly.

What it means for Nvidia and the investigation

Huang has not spoken publicly since his reinstatement on Monday. His attorney said the matter remained before federal investigators and declined further comment. The espionage investigation concerns alleged transfers of chip design data to Chinese entities that investigators believe took place over a period of years. Nvidia said in a statement that the company cooperates fully with all federal inquiries and that it supports Huang's return as the person best positioned to lead the company. Nvidia stock closed Tuesday at approximately $85, down from Monday's close of $89 but still well above the $58 low recorded after Huang's resignation on November 13.
