Zelensky says U.S. proposed Donbas-for-NATO deal

Ukrainian President Zelensky said U.S. negotiators asked for the first time whether Ukraine would withdraw from the Donbas in exchange for NATO membership. He said the American side raised the idea after the Abu Dhabi talks ended Wednesday. Zelensky rejected any outright surrender of territory but said he could put a formal proposal to the Ukrainian people.

Russia has long insisted Ukrainian NATO membership is a red line. Moscow also demanded this week that Finland be expelled from NATO as a condition for any broader settlement. The next round of talks has not been scheduled.

SpaceX catches Starship upper stage in first-ever tower catch

SpaceX's Starship Flight 15 completed its mission Friday night, with the upper stage caught by the launch tower's mechanical arms at Starbase, Texas after 14 orbits over roughly 22 hours. It is the first time the upper stage has been recovered intact. The booster aborted its own catch attempt, and SpaceX said it is investigating why.

The vehicle carried a batch of Starlink satellites. SpaceX called the catch a step toward making both stages of Starship fully reusable. The rocket launched on November 13 from Boca Chica.

Two more tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy said two oil tankers struck naval mines south of the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday and caught fire. The IRGC said the vessels were traveling along an unauthorized route with their navigation and identification systems switched off. No casualties were confirmed immediately.

Three tankers caught fire in the same area on November 12. The U.S. and several European governments have called the incidents deliberate acts of maritime sabotage. Iran has not formally claimed responsibility for placing mines but said the vessels violated its declared exclusion zone.

UPS cargo plane and Copa Airlines jet collide at Miami airport

A UPS Boeing 767 and a Copa Airlines Boeing 737 collided on a taxiway at Miami International Airport Saturday night. The wingtip of the Copa aircraft struck the nose of the UPS plane while both were taxiing. The FAA said air traffic controllers had instructed the UPS crew to give way. Two people were treated for minor injuries at the scene and did not need hospital care.

All 166 passengers and six crew aboard the Copa flight were reported in good condition. The FAA is investigating. Airport operations were not disrupted. The collision comes days after a nationwide FAA ground stop that left thousands of flights delayed or canceled.

Iran rejects IAEA demand to halt uranium enrichment

Iran's government rejected a demand by the International Atomic Energy Agency to suspend uranium enrichment, calling the agency's statements politically motivated. Iran's foreign ministry said the IAEA was engaging in what it described as nontechnical speculation and acting outside its mandate.

The IAEA board of governors has been pressing Iran to cap its enrichment levels ahead of the planned Trump-Xi-Putin summit in Shenzhen on November 19, where nuclear stockpiles are expected to be discussed. Iran's current enrichment level stands at 60 percent purity, below weapons-grade but above what is needed for civilian energy use.
