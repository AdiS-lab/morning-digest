France: 600,000 protested

back to digest · home

On November 5, roughly 600,000 people took part in nationwide strikes and protests across France. Riot police fired tear gas and charged demonstrators in Lyon.

Scale in context

France has a long history of mass protests. Here's how this one compares.

600,000 is large but not unprecedented. The 2023 pension reform protests drew over a million at their peak. The gilets jaunes peaked around 300,000. France treats protest as a civic institution in a way most countries don't.
