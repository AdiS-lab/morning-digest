Democrats plan Trump investigations

back to digest · home

Democrats announced plans for comprehensive congressional investigations into the president's family and close associates, starting when they take control of both chambers in early 2027.

This is the pattern

The party that wins midterms uses subpoena power to investigate the president. It happens every time.

Every president since Clinton has faced this. The opposition wins the midterms, gains committee chairs, and launches investigations. Some produce results. Some are political theater. All of them consume enormous amounts of the administration's time and attention.

What's different this time

Democrats control both chambers. In 2018 they only had the House. Having the Senate means they also control confirmations, which gives them leverage over cabinet replacements and judicial appointments. The Alito situation is directly connected to this.
