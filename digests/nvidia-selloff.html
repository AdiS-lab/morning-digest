Nvidia lost $450 billion in one day

back to digest · home

On November 5, 2026, Nvidia's stock dropped more than 9%. That wiped out roughly $450 billion in market capitalization. To put that in perspective:

  • $450 billion is larger than the entire market cap of JPMorgan Chase
  • It's roughly the GDP of Norway
  • Nvidia lost more value in one day than most companies are worth in total

It wasn't just Nvidia. The whole AI trade unwound at once.

The chart above shows the single-day percentage drops across major chip and AI infrastructure stocks. AMD and Micron both fell harder in percentage terms. Nvidia's drop was the largest in absolute dollars because of its massive valuation.

Why it happened

Three things converged:

  1. Valuations were stretched. Nvidia was trading at over 40x forward earnings. The market had priced in years of growth that hadn't happened yet.
  2. Political backlash. The midterm results gave Democrats momentum to push back on AI infrastructure spending. A "national data center moratorium" went from fringe idea to serious policy proposal overnight.
  3. The spending question. Companies are pouring billions into AI infrastructure, but the revenue to justify it hasn't materialized. Google announced a $17 billion data center the same day its chip suppliers cratered. Investors noticed the contradiction.

The paradox

The companies buying Nvidia's chips are spending more than ever. Google: $17 billion data center. Meta: billions in bond markets for AI infrastructure. The demand side is accelerating. The stock is crashing anyway. The market is saying: we believe you're spending the money, we just don't believe you'll make it back.

AI infrastructure spending keeps climbing even as the stocks of the companies building it fall. At some point one of these lines has to be wrong.
