The job market is fine, actually

back to digest · home

The headline numbers are good. Unemployment at 4.1%, claims at 189,000. But there's a split between what the numbers say and what the AI industry promised.

Unemployment rate, 2024-2026

Stable around 4%. No spike, no crash. The labor market absorbed AI without the mass displacement that was predicted. But also without the productivity boom that was promised.

The Bank of England finding

UK firms are using AI to cut hiring, not fire people. Open positions that would have been filled are just not being posted. The productivity gain: 0.5% over three years. For context, normal productivity growth in a healthy economy is 1-2% per year.

The promise was that AI would transform productivity. The reality so far is a rounding error. It might still happen. But three years in, the data says otherwise.
