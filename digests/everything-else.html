Tesla winding down Cybertruck

Production is being halted due to weak sales. The truck launched in late 2023 to massive hype. Sales never matched it. The angular design was polarizing. Build quality was inconsistent. Price was too high for most truck buyers. Tesla is quietly moving on.

RealPage: $360 million

RealPage made software that landlords used to coordinate rent prices. The DOJ argued this was algorithmic price-fixing. They settled for $360 million in restitution to tenants. The case matters because it sets a precedent: using an algorithm to coordinate prices is still price-fixing.

FAA ditching floppy disks

The Federal Aviation Administration still uses floppy disks in some air traffic control systems. They've announced a plan to phase them out by 2028. The systems work. They've worked for decades. The problem is finding replacement hardware. Nobody makes floppy drives anymore.
