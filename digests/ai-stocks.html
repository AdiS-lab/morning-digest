Chip stocks started sliding the day after the midterms. Democrat Gina Hinojosa won the Texas governor's race. She has proposed a moratorium on new state data centers. Bernie Sanders has called for a national one. Investors read that as a threat to the data center buildout. See the midterms page.

Nvidia, day by day

Thursday was the worst day. Nvidia lost more than $440 billion in market value. Friday brought a partial bounce. It did not erase the losses. Earlier coverage is on the Nvidia sell-off page.

Thursday's damage

All 11 market sectors fell on Thursday. That included defensive ones like utilities and consumer staples.

Other worries

About 600,000 Nvidia H200 chips bound for China are stuck in Hong Kong. Import licenses are delayed, per Reuters. Nvidia had no company news. No customer has reported canceling an order. Nvidia reports earnings on November 17.
