Trump offers Dana White the press secretary job

back to digest · home

Trump posted the offer on Truth Social on Saturday. He called White a fighter who would bring a new style to the role. He said the offer extended to UFC fighters as assistants, "even though some are not from our Country."

CNN reported, citing a source, that White declined. White has not said so. He posted a photo of himself behind the White House briefing lectern with the caption "On to the next chapter." He says he will meet Trump on Monday. One aide said Trump was joking.

A busy week for White House staffing

The offer came one day after Trump named Stephen Miller chief of staff. Both changes take shape Monday. See the Miller page.

The job has been unstable since August

Karoline Leavitt held the job for about a year and a half. She left in August to spend more time with her young family. Her replacement, Katie Zacharia, was fired after one rocky briefing. That is the shortest stint on record.
